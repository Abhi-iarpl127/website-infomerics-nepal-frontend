About Infomerics Group

Infomerics Valuation and Rating Limited (Erstwhile Infomerics Valuation and Rating Pvt. Ltd.) is a SEBI registered and RBI accredited Credit Rating Agency. Infomerics employs credit rating models developed in-house with the aid of inter-alia, ML-based frameworks to estimate probability of default.
